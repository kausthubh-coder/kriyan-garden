Onboarding, step 1 of 5: areas

Every step has the same frame: wordmark and "Use sample data" at the top, the question on the left, a live preview on the right that updates as the person answers. The preview is built from the real app components, not a picture. One primary button, "Continue", 46px tall; "Back" and "Skip" are quiet text buttons. Area rows have their colour dot; clicking a name edits it in place; the close icon removes it.

Onboarding, step 3 of 5: classes and meetings

Added classes are rows. The form is one line of name, weekday toggles starting on Monday, a start and end time written as 24-hour text fields (type "10" or "1015"; never the browser's 12-hour time box), and an optional place. The preview shows them on the timeline for the next day they occur.

Step 2, "What are you working on?": under each area heading, a row per project or course and an "Add a project or course" row; the preview lists areas with their projects. Step 4, "What are you aiming for?": a title field, area chips, target chips (End of the month, In 3 months, Pick a day) and measure chips (Tasks done, A number, Milestones); the preview is the goal card with its pace bar. Phone width: the preview moves under the question and is 260px tall; "Continue" is full width and pinned above the bottom inset.

Settings: areas, with one row open

The section index is a plain list with a soft fill on the current section, not outlined boxes. Each section is a list of rows. A row opens its editor inline, the same pattern as the task panel. There are no "Save", "Move up", "Move down" and "Delete" buttons on every row: changes to a field save when it loses focus, order changes by dragging the grip (arrow keys when the grip is focused), and "Delete" is inside the open row.
