Your day on one timeline.

Kriyan is an open-source planner for school, business and life. Tasks with a time sit on the timeline. Tasks without one wait beside it. Length is optional.

Live demo renders here: the real Day view, 1180 by 640, side rail visible, chips on one row.

Try it. This is the real app with sample data, and nothing you do here is saved.

Type it the way you would say it.

Am I on pace?

Every goal shows where you are and where you should be today. Every deadline shows the time it needs against the time you have, so you can make room before the date arrives.

Plan with the AI you already use.

Kriyan has an MCP server. Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor, approve access once, and ask it to plan your day, move tasks or check a deadline. It sees the same tasks and goals you do.

Or from the terminal

npx kriyan add "essay fri 5pm #econ 2h" npx kriyan today
ClaudeClaude CodeChatGPTCursorVS Code
# Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector Name Kriyan Server URL https://app.kriyan.app/mcp # Then ask "What is on my day? Move the essay to Friday at 5."

Sign in happens in your browser. Kriyan never sees your AI account, and the AI only gets the access you approve.

Kriyan on your phone.

The Android app has the same timeline, quick add and goals, with reminders that arrive even when a task was added on the web or by an AI.
