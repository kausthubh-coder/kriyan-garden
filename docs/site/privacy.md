# Privacy

Kriyan stores the information needed to provide your planner. This policy describes the intended hosted service and is subject to production configuration review before launch.

## What is stored

Clerk manages your account identity, including your email address and sign-in information. Convex stores your planner data: profile preferences, areas, projects, courses, tasks, notes, goals, milestones, timetable events and habits. The Android service may store a push token to send reminders when that feature is available.

## Where it is stored

Account identity is processed by Clerk and planner data is stored in Convex. Vercel hosts the web service. The hosted Convex region is not recorded in this checkout and has not been verified; the operator must disclose the selected region before production launch. Do not assume the service uses a particular country or region.

## Who can see it

Your signed-in app and authorized AI/API clients can access your planner. Other users cannot access your rows through the application. The operator can access the database for maintenance and support. Clerk, Convex and Vercel process data as service providers under their own service terms. Do not store information you cannot entrust to these providers.

## Export and deletion

Settings provides planner-data deletion through Danger zone > Reset everything. Resetting your planner does not delete your Clerk identity. Settings > Account provides Clerk account controls. Email [kausthubh2007@gmail.com](mailto:kausthubh2007@gmail.com) for an export, account deletion help or privacy requests. Deletion from active data may not immediately remove copies held in provider backups or required operational logs.

## Analytics and advertising

There are no ads and no added analytics beyond Vercel's default operational logging and platform diagnostics. Authentication uses necessary session storage and cookies through Clerk. The public demo holds sample data in memory and does not save your edits when you reload.
