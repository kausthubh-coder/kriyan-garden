# Getting started

Kriyan is an open-source planner for school, business and life. Your day is a timeline with a tray beside it. Task length is optional.

## Try a day

[Open the public demo](/demo) to explore sample tasks without signing in. Add a task, drag it onto the timeline, complete it or open its details. Changes stay in memory and disappear when you reload. The demo uses the same components as the web app.

## Create your planner

[Sign up](https://app.kriyan.app/sign-up), then choose your areas. School, Business and Life are suggested starting points. Rename them, add projects or courses, and enter classes or fixed meetings in your timetable. You can skip setup steps and return to Settings later.

## Add a task

Choose Add task or press N. Try `gym tomorrow 7am`, `essay fri #econ 2h` or `call amma`. The chips show the day, time, area and optional length before you add it. A #tag selects an existing area, project or course. [Read the quick-add grammar](/docs/quick-add).

## Plan your day

Tasks with a time sit on the timeline. Tasks without one wait in Any time. Tasks with no date wait in No date yet. Drag a task onto the timeline to schedule it, or open its details and choose a day and time. Move a timed task to another slot or resize one with a length. Task details provide the same controls with a keyboard or on a phone.

## Leave length optional

Without a length, a timed task is a marker at its start time. With a length, it occupies a block and counts toward planned hours. Kriyan does not require a length. Your capacity is a planning preference, not a requirement to fill every hour.

## Check your pace

Goals can track linked tasks, a number or milestones. Set a target date to see a marker for where you should be today. The Day and Week views show deadlines and the time you have free. List groups tasks by area; Week shows the load across seven days.

## Your other surfaces
